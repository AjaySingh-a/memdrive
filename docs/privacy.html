Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

memdrive is a free, open-source program that runs on your own computer. It backs up the data of claude-mem to your own Google Drive and restores it from there. This policy explains what data memdrive handles and how.

The short version

memdrive has no server. We do not collect, receive, store, sell or share any of your data. Your backups go directly from your computer to your Google Drive, and nobody else, including the memdrive developers, can access them.

Google user data memdrive accesses

When you connect memdrive, you grant it the Google Drive drive.file permission. With this permission memdrive can only access files and folders that memdrive itself created in your Google Drive. It cannot see, read or change any other file in your Drive.

memdrive uses this access only to:

  • create a folder named memdrive in your Drive, with one sub-folder per computer;
  • upload backup files of your claude-mem data into those folders;
  • list and download those backup files when you restore;
  • delete old memdrive backup files according to your retention settings.

What is stored, and where

  • On your computer: your Google sign-in token, in ~/.memdrive/credentials.json, readable only by your user account, plus memdrive's settings and log files in the same folder.
  • In your Google Drive: the backup files. A backup contains claude-mem's databases, vector index and settings from your computer. If you put API keys in claude-mem's settings, they are included in your backup.
  • Anywhere else: nothing. memdrive has no analytics, telemetry or tracking.

How Google user data is used and shared

Data obtained through Google APIs is used only to provide memdrive's backup and restore features to you, and is never transferred to anyone else. memdrive's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data and access

  • Run memdrive disconnect to stop backups and revoke memdrive's access, or remove memdrive at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete the memdrive folder in Google Drive to remove all backups.
  • Delete the ~/.memdrive folder on your computer to remove local settings.

Children

memdrive is a developer tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

Changes to this policy are published on this page and in the project's GitHub repository, with the date above updated.
